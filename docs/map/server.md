## Projects
- [vite.config.js](../../vite.config.js): the whole back end: dev-server middleware for every /api route, inline feeds (FRED relay, S&P screener, fear & greed, AI usage, memory prices …), proxies for CBOE, FRED, OpenRouter, Zillow, CFTC, and the /api/chat assistant (Anthropic SDK)
- [server/](../../server): one module per feed, each `createX({ dir, keys })` with memory, disk and rebuild caching
- [server/usPulse.js](../../server/usPulse.js): U.S. Economy landing feed (~50 FRED series, leading indicators, consumer, debt)
- [server/intlPulse.js](../../server/intlPulse.js): International landing feed (13 economies, Big Mac index)
- [server/policyRates.js](../../server/policyRates.js): BIS central bank policy rates (~40 banks, monthly history, daily latest) — /api/policy-rates and the base layer of /api/cb-rates (vite.config.js), which the pulse and Forex read
- [server/worldEconomy.js](../../server/worldEconomy.js): /api/world-economy — IMF WEO, World Bank WLD, BIS (total credit, world house prices, central-bank assets, debt securities, USD FX), IMF COFER, CPB World Trade Monitor, nine economies' broad money (FRED, ECB, Eastmoney, BoJ, BoE, SNB, BoC, RBA, BCB), VT nowcast, Savills/UBS anchors; rewrites its tracked seed when IMF/COFER data change
- [server/oilMarket.js](../../server/oilMarket.js): /api/oil-market — EIA API v2 (spot prices, frozen NYMEX futures to Apr 2024, weekly stocks/supply/demand/utilization, monthly production by state, STEO) and CME WTI/Brent settlement curves with a daily archive (personal use; gitignored cache only)
- [server/cotReport.js](../../server/cotReport.js): /api/cot-report — CFTC Disaggregated (kh3c-gbw2) and Traders in Financial Futures (yw9f-hn96) combined reports for 35 markets plus Yahoo weekly prices; COT index, flows, price/OI quadrant, top-4 concentration, forward-return backtest
- [server/aiBuildout.js](../../server/aiBuildout.js): /api/ai-buildout — funding loop vs productivity scoring: SEC XBRL (capex, OCF, debt proceeds, backlog, finance-lease adds) for the capexReturns hyperscalers, Census construction (data centers) and BTOS, SF Fed TFP, BEA via FRED, plus the Dark Output and AI pulse feeds; rules and hand inputs in data/ai-tracker/ (see docs/ai-tracker.md)
- [server/magicFormula.js](../../server/magicFormula.js): Greenblatt's Magic Formula across all U.S. stocks: the FMP screener universe (daily) and each company's quarterly income statements and balance sheet, fetched in the background at 150 requests a minute, pausing when FMP refuses (a cold build takes about 35 minutes, then a company is refetched when a new quarter should be out); ranked at four market-value floors; one fetch loop per process across dev-server restarts
- [server/guruTracker.js](../../server/guruTracker.js): /api/gurus, /api/gurus/manager?slug= and /api/gurus/held, the Gurus tab: reads the Research Database's 13F summary (data/generated/gurus.json), one file per manager (data/generated/gurus/managers/<slug>.json, positions named from gurus/holdings.json) and the most widely held names (from holdings.json), all written each Monday by its scripts/gurus.py, from the checkout in RESEARCH_DB_DIR or next to this folder, or with RESEARCH_DB_TOKEN from GitHub (private repo, RESEARCH_DB_BRANCH, cached an hour). Holds no data of its own
- [server/researchDb.js](../../server/researchDb.js): the one reader for files in the private Research Database (a checkout in RESEARCH_DB_DIR or next to this folder, else GitHub with RESEARCH_DB_TOKEN): used by the guru tracker and by the AI section's company-level inputs, which name holdings and so are not kept in this repository
- [server/taiwanRevenue.js](../../server/taiwanRevenue.js): /api/ai-supply-chain, AI Economy → Supply Chain: monthly revenue of the Taiwan-listed AI hardware chain from the exchange's Market Observation Post System pages (Big5 HTML, one page per market per month; each past month fetched once into the gitignored taiwan-revenue.json, the first build takes several minutes). Company list: the Research Database's data/curated/ai-supply-chain.json
- [server/aiCredit.js](../../server/aiCredit.js): /api/ai-credit, AI Economy → Build-Out: the AI credit basket, each issuer's median spread over the fitted Treasury curve from the corporate tape's own snapshots (no requests of its own; personal use like the tape), changes over the same bonds, convertibles by price; each day's reading kept in the gitignored ai-credit.json. Issuer list: the Research Database's data/curated/ai-credit-basket.json
- [server/aiFlows.js](../../server/aiFlows.js): /api/ai-flows, AI Economy → Models & Labs: who pays whom for compute, from the Research Database's data/curated/ai-flows.json, with each supplier's reported backlog (its backlog.json) set beside the commitments. Holds no data
- [server/computeBalance.js](../../server/computeBalance.js): /api/compute-balance, AI Economy → Compute: shortage or glut. Eleven signals graded by printed rules from feeds other modules keep (H100 and A100 contract prices on the year, spot against contract, the futures curve, launchable listings, the spot auction, cloud spot discounts, the resale index, a lab's revenue per compute dollar, suppliers' backlog from the Research Database, DRAM spot), the verdict as a count, and the growth race (yearly multiples of demand and supply, not netted; demand includes business token spending from the Ramp AI Index archive, its month-on-month readings compounded to a year and flagged until twelve are on file). Daily verdicts in the gitignored compute-balance.json
- [server/crossAsset.js](../../server/crossAsset.js): /api/cross-asset, Cockpit → Four markets: stocks, bonds, currencies and commodities read together, weekly since 1999. Twelve signals (FRED rates, spreads, Fed balance sheet, dollar, yen, copper; FMP closes for sector and asset ETFs) scored as 13-week moves against their own past volatility, averaged into a liquidity and a growth axis, a regime cell, a clarity score and stance, a trend read per market, and a playbook of what each market did next in each regime; plus about 60 instruments under the four markets (sector, style and region ETFs; Treasury yields and bond funds; a dozen currencies against the dollar and bitcoin; metals, energy and baskets) with a breakout read (new 52-week high or low, a 40-week-average cross, the range edge), blended momentum ranked across all of them, relative strength against the S&P 500 for stocks, and beats-cash. ETF closes kept in the gitignored cross-asset-prices.json. The yield-curve regime and Fed-turn read come from server/curveTurn.js on the same grid with the latest close as a partial last week
- [server/curveTurn.js](../../server/curveTurn.js): pure functions for Four markets: the yield-curve regime (bull or bear, steepening or flattening, over 13 and 4 weeks from the 2-year and 10-year) and the Fed turn (in a selloff of 8% or more, two of three tells, the 2-year down 35 bp in 8 weeks, the dollar rolling over, bull steepening, is the Fed blinking; none with no cuts priced and off the zero bound is holding firm), each past episode with the S&P 13, 26 and 52 weeks on, and what each market did after each curve regime
- [server/regimeAnalogs.js](../../server/regimeAnalogs.js): /api/regime-analogs, Cockpit → when has the market looked like this before. Every four weeks since 1999 the past 13 weeks become a vector of sixteen cross-asset readings (equities, rates, credit, currencies, commodities, volatility; group-weighted, in standard deviations); similarity is 75% cosine plus 25% magnitude; the ten closest earlier nodes with what the S&P and the Baa spread did next, a rule-based regime label, similarity to a fixed list of stress episodes, a deterministic force layout for the map, and a backtest of whether analogs called direction. Uses the daily inputs crossAsset.js keeps (its inputs()) plus FRED VIXCLS; nothing stored
- [server/liquidity.js](../../server/liquidity.js): /api/liquidity, U.S. Economy → Rates & Fed → Liquidity: 21 gauges in six layers (central bank, money markets, money and bank credit, Treasury market, credit, global dollar), weekly since 2000 from FRED (H.4.1, H.8, SOFR and its percentiles and volume, the reserve rate with IOER before 2021, M2, the loan officer survey, term premium, ICE and Moody's spreads, NFCI, the dollar, ECB and Bank of Japan assets), each graded by a printed rule; a composite of the flow gauges, its 13-week impulse, a lead test against the S&P 500, and the net-liquidity series (its one home)
- [server/rampAi.js](../../server/rampAi.js): /api/ramp-ai, AI Economy → Tokens: the Ramp AI Index read from Ramp's page (its server-rendered payload carries every monthly series: adoption overall, by lab, size and industry; spend per employee; AI's share of business spend; plus the latest week's token shares, blended price and growth, written only as a table and prose). At most twice a day; FRED's RAMPAI* series as the fallback; the cache and a weekly token archive in the gitignored ramp-ai.json
- [server/tensions/checks/ai-2027.js](../../server/tensions/checks/ai-2027.js): the AI Economy → Tensions card that sets the AI 2027 scenario's dated numbers (data/ai/ai-2027.json) beside filed and disclosed figures: lead-lab revenue, compute bill and the unit cost of compute against the scenario's path, with the direction of each miss read as shortage or glut. A check may now carry `labels` to rename the board's three evidence sections
- [server/inflationDecomp.js](../../server/inflationDecomp.js): /api/inflation-decomp, U.S. Economy → Inflation: each component's contribution to headline and core PCE inflation by month (twelve-month, three-month annualised, one-month), from FRED's monthly BEA price indexes and nominal spending through the shared cache. Six pieces that sum to the published rate (services ex energy and housing, housing, durable goods, other core goods, food, energy); housing and other core goods are differences of contributions between published aggregates
- [server/ideas.js](../../server/ideas.js): /api/ideas, the Ideas page's three buckets: Magic Formula stocks, special situations (the scorer's A and B grades plus open odd-lot tenders) and fixed-income rules (FRED curve, TIPS, ICE BofA spreads, mortgage rates; bond closed-end fund discounts). `?symbol=` looks up one stock's rank, `?only=stocks` returns the stock bucket alone. - [fred-cache.json](../../fred-cache.json): shared FRED observation cache (gitignored)
- [tickers.json](../../tickers.json): the saved watchlist behind /api/tickers (gitignored)
- [special-dealbook.json](../../special-dealbook.json): the personal Deal Book behind /api/special-dealbook (gitignored)
- News Desk state at the root, gitignored because it names the watchlist: `news-desk.json` (items and Claude's reads), `news-desk-meta.json` (the brief, feed health, the last run), `news-usage.json` (Claude spend per day), `news-profile.json` (company names and CIKs; other names for a company go in its `aliases` list)
- Options → Market state at the root, gitignored because it names the watchlist: `options-market-scan.json` (the last scan), `options-market-history.json` (each name's daily IV, skew and put/call, 400 days; an archive, never written over when unreadable) and `options-market-indices.json` (the Cboe index histories)
- Other feed caches (us-pulse.json, redfin.json, gpu-economics.json, accounting-flags.json, sin-monitor.json, folly-markets.json, intelligence-mw-cache.json …) are gitignored; see [.gitignore](../../.gitignore)
- Tracked archives these modules append to: [data snapshots](data.md)

## Skills
- `npm run dev`: start the dev server, which is also the API server (port 5180 or `$PORT`)
- `curl localhost:5180/api/tickers`: cheap health check (reads a local file, no upstream calls)
- `curl "localhost:5180/api/news-desk?auto=0"`: the News Desk payload without starting a refresh

## Memory
- The header comment of each server/ module documents its sources, scores and cache TTLs
- [docs/ideas.md](../ideas.md): the Ideas recommender's buckets, rules and known limits

## Routines
Each feed rebuilds on request once its TTL (2h to 30 days) expires. The one timer is the News Desk's: it refreshes hourly, but only while the desk has been opened on this computer in the past 24 hours; between midnight and 5 am it only scores, and briefs follow the clock (after 6 am, noon and 4 pm) (server/newsDesk/index.js).
