## Projects
- [index.html](../../index.html): the page shell Vite serves at /
- [src/main.jsx](../../src/main.jsx): React entry point
- [src/App.jsx](../../src/App.jsx): the shell: tab groups (Today: Cockpit, Regime Map, News Desk and a link out to Ideas; Valuation, Income, Macro, Themes), data loading, data-health status. `?view=` opens any tab; `?view=stocks&sub=…` and `?view=economy&sub=…` open a sub-view (StocksTab.jsx, USEconomyTab.jsx), and `?view=stocks&symbol=…` a stock page
- [ideas.html](../../ideas.html): the second page shell, served at /ideas (vite.config.js rewrites the path and lists it as a build input)
- [src/ideas/](../../src/ideas): the Ideas page: IdeasApp.jsx (shortlist; Magic Formula stocks by sector with a market-value floor and a rank lookup; special situations; fixed income; each idea links to its evidence on the dashboard) and ideas.css (its own light and dark tokens; shares the dashboard's theme setting)
- [src/tabs/NewsDeskTab.jsx](../../src/tabs/NewsDeskTab.jsx): the News Desk: the daily brief, news by holding and by category with Claude's reads, proposed rows for the data/ai tables, the clip bookmarklet and paste box, feed health
- [src/tabs/](../../src/tabs): one component per tab and sub-tab (Overview cockpit, Rates, CPI, U.S. Economy, SFC Model, Tightening …)
- [src/tabs/stocks/](../../src/tabs/stocks): S&P 500 screener, people screener, research sheet, debt & cash (with optimal debt), accounting (Beneish, Montier, accruals, footnotes, filings), technicals, valuation (cost of capital, industry yardstick, story valuation), value book, special situations (with the Odd lots board: issuer tenders that buy holders of under 100 shares in full, priced as a 99-share trade), closed-end funds (ClosedEndFunds.jsx: discount screener, a fund's discount against its own range, distributions, and a monthly backtest run in the browser on /api/cef's panel), sin & folly; the biggest company ÷ GDP (TopCompanyGdp.jsx, on S&P Overview)
- [src/tabs/options/](../../src/tabs/options): the Options tab's views. MarketView.jsx is the landing page (VIX family and term structure, put/call, SKEW/VVIX, cross-asset vol, the volatility-and-skew board, skew curves, premium traded, unusual activity) on /api/options-market via the shared hook marketData.js; SymbolNav.jsx steps through the watchlist / ETFs / S&P 500 (by size or sector) with ‹ › and ← →, prefetching the neighbours' chains; MiniFans.jsx draws small-multiple implied ranges from the scan above ExpectationsView; StrategiesView.jsx is the strategy rail, explainer, builder (payoff over the risk-neutral density on one price axis) and cross-name screener on src/lib/optionStrategies.js; ui.jsx holds the shared dense components and the validated --oc-* chart palette (OptionsResearch.css). Also income, expectations, volatility history, watchlist ladder
- [src/tabs/GurusTab.jsx](../../src/tabs/GurusTab.jsx): Valuation → Gurus (`?view=gurus`), the Research Database's guru tracker drawn in the dashboard's palette, managers first. Managers view: the roster (sortable, searchable, by style; `?manager=<slug>` deep-links a page), a manager's page (twelve quarters of book value, theme mix against the prior quarter, this quarter's new/added/trimmed/sold, every position with weight, shares, estimated cost, return since and held since, option lines) and the most widely held names. Consensus view: builds against sells, theme flows over nine quarters, exits, guru moves in your own names, names bought together, option lines. A ticker opens its stock page; "Story ↗" and the Research Database links open the full tracker there (RESEARCH_DB_URL)
- [src/tabs/BondsTab.jsx](../../src/tabs/BondsTab.jsx): Income → Bonds (`?view=bonds&sub=treasuries|rates|credit`), the fixed-income home: Treasuries (every issue on the fitted curve, rich and cheap against neighbouring issues, TIPS breakevens, graded auctions and the calendar, the full list); Rates & mortgages (RatesTab.jsx) and Corporate credit (DebtMarketTab.jsx), both moved from U.S. Economy in Oct 2026, which keeps the Fed balance sheet and tightening monitor under "Fed" and hands drill-downs to the moved views over to Bonds
- [src/tabs/CorporateBondsView.jsx](../../src/tabs/CorporateBondsView.jsx): Bonds → Corporate bonds (`?view=bonds&sub=corporates`): most active, the biggest moves, bonds trading below 70, a fallen-angel watch (split ratings, recent BB+ / Ba1 actions, investment grade priced like junk) and the issuer watchlist with each bond's spread over Treasuries
- [src/tabs/history/](../../src/tabs/history): the Historical tab's long-run sections (interest rates, inflation, growth), under HistoricalTab.jsx with the asset-returns view
- [src/tabs/realEstate/](../../src/tabs/realEstate): market map (states, metros, counties), market profile, metro comparison, rental pricing, refinancing; Global (GlobalHousing.jsx: ~60 countries' real house prices, valuation, household debt, world map, commercial property)
- [src/tabs/ai/](../../src/tabs/ai): capex returns, GPU economics, intelligence per megawatt, Nebius spot (NebiusSpot.jsx, on Compute), GPU markets (GpuMarkets.jsx: the price ladder, CME forward curve, availability, a year of prices, used-GPU index, hyperscaler spot by region), token estimates, token spot; Dark Output (DarkOutput.jsx: SemiAnalysis fingerprints of AI work the statistics miss)
- [src/tabs/intl/](../../src/tabs/intl): the International tab's five views — international pulse (IntlPulseTab.jsx, also exports the Big Mac panel Forex uses), Central Banks & Rates (CentralBanks.jsx: BIS policy rates for ~40 banks), Forex (ForexTab.jsx with TradeFlows.jsx), Share of the World and China; GlobalImbalances.jsx and LastMile.jsx are shared pieces; Share of the World (ShareOfWorld.jsx: Li Lu's wealth-as-share-of-world-output lens) and China (ChinaMonitor.jsx: objective vs cost)
- [src/tabs/WorldTab.jsx](../../src/tabs/WorldTab.jsx): the World tab — the globe as one economy: balance sheet (real estate, equities, bonds, broad money, central-bank assets, reserves, debt by sector, household wealth) in $tn and × world GDP, market cap ÷ GDP, money, trade, saving vs investment, imbalances, people, IMF member table
- [src/tabs/OilMarket.jsx](../../src/tabs/OilMarket.jsx): Commodities → Oil — prices and the CME curve, timespreads since 1985, product prices and cracks, U.S. stocks vs their five-year range, SPR, refining, supply and demand, the EIA STEO balance and producers
- [src/tabs/CftcSubTab.jsx](../../src/tabs/CftcSubTab.jsx): Commodities → CFTC Positioning — the Commitments of Traders explained: primer and glossary, a 35-market scoreboard (COT index, flows, price + open interest), per-market detail with a plain-English read, and a crowding backtest
- [src/tabs/InflationDecomposition.jsx](../../src/tabs/InflationDecomposition.jsx): on U.S. Economy → Inflation (CpiTab.jsx), under the momentum table: what PCE inflation is made of, stacked contributions by month reaching the published rate, headline or core, three windows, and a table of each piece's weight, own rate, contribution and distance from a 2% pace
- [src/tabs/ai/BuildOut.jsx](../../src/tabs/ai/BuildOut.jsx): AI Economy → Build-Out — synthesis line, the three to watch, panels A (funding loop), B (boundary), C (productivity), the hand-kept event log and the thresholds in force
- [src/tabs/ai/SupplyChainRevenue.jsx](../../src/tabs/ai/SupplyChainRevenue.jsx): AI Economy → Supply Chain (with the CoWoS/HBM ceiling and memory prices, moved here from Compute): monthly revenue along the Taiwan AI hardware chain, one growth panel per link and a company table
- [src/tabs/ai/LabEconomics.jsx](../../src/tabs/ai/LabEconomics.jsx) and [ComputeFlows.jsx](../../src/tabs/ai/ComputeFlows.jsx): AI Economy → Models & Labs: a lab's filed income statement (costs by what was bought against revenue, margin after all compute, serving against research compute; src/data/labFinancials.json) and who pays whom (commitments as bands from payer to supplier, each supplier's backlog against its named payers, the agreements with their basis)
- [src/tabs/ai/AiCredit.jsx](../../src/tabs/ai/AiCredit.jsx): AI Economy → Build-Out, under the funding loop: the AI credit basket, issuers by tier on one log spread scale, and the builders' convertibles by price
- [src/tabs/ai/AiDollars.jsx](../../src/tabs/ai/AiDollars.jsx): AI Economy → Models & Labs: the AI dollar ledger: bars for what customers pay, what capital puts in and the survey cross-checks; every company behind the revenue tiers; capex guidance by company; venture funding and the largest rounds; the surveys with their restatements
- [src/tabs/ai/AiLabor.jsx](../../src/tabs/ai/AiLabor.jsx): AI Economy → Dark Output: are payrolls paying for AI: monthly AI-attributed cuts and their share of all announced cuts, quarterly restructuring filings, payroll freed against the labs' new revenue, and the annual totals
- [src/tabs/ai/TokenLedger.jsx](../../src/tabs/ai/TokenLedger.jsx): AI Economy → Tokens: the token ledger: a log-scale chart of every disclosed token volume by company and scope, the table of what each counts with its source, trillion-token customer counts, and what inference platforms have said about their scale
- [src/tabs/ai/TokenLadder.jsx](../../src/tabs/ai/TokenLadder.jsx): AI Economy → Tokens: the token ladder on a log scale (typical token, input or output), three reference open models with provider prices and margins over derived cost, and who serves the open models; the cost derivation itself stays on Compute (token spot)
- [src/tabs/ai/TokenTrends.jsx](../../src/tabs/ai/TokenTrends.jsx): AI Economy → Tokens, two panels: QualityPrice (step lines of the cheapest model at or above a fixed level: Epoch's benchmark levels to early 2025, and this year's index levels) and TokenElasticity (price, volume and implied spend on OpenRouter; the mix behind the price; the three reads; Vercel's labs)
- [src/tabs/ai/JevonsTracker.jsx](../../src/tabs/ai/JevonsTracker.jsx): AI Economy → Tokens, under the elasticity test: Jevons by lab on OpenRouter. Verdict tiles by share of tokens; for the selected row, price, tokens and spend by day (first week = 100, log) and a log-log scatter of each day's tokens against its price with the fitted β; a sortable table of every lab (tokens, share, spend, days, price first → last week, the three changes, β, R², verdict). Click a row to chart it
- [src/tabs/ai/GpuPredictionMarkets.jsx](../../src/tabs/ai/GpuPredictionMarkets.jsx): AI Economy → Compute → GPU prices: what Polymarket and Kalshi expect a GPU-hour to rent for. Tiles pick the chip (H100, H200, B200, B300, A100, RTX 5090); the chart joins the Ornn index's daily history to one step per contract month at each venue's median (Polymarket with the middle half of its odds shaded; Kalshi solid from quotes, dashed from last trades, shaded where resting orders support a range; CME marks in grey where there are any); the table puts the venues side by side, and drops a venue's columns for chips it does not list. `?view=ai&sub=compute&section=prices` opens the section (any Compute section id works)
- [src/tabs/ai/MemoryPrices.jsx](../../src/tabs/ai/MemoryPrices.jsx): AI Economy → Memory (its own sub-tab; replaces the old MemoryPricesPanel on Supply Chain): the dollars-per-GB ladder from HBM to NAND, headline parts indexed, DDR4 over DDR5, the board of every quoted part, contract tables and quarterly contract forecasts, HBM figures and makers' reported price and bit changes
- AI Economy → Compute (ComputeTab in AIEconomyTab.jsx) is six sections that open and close (shortage or glut, GPU prices, GPU economics, tokens from GPUs, power, forecasts), each with a one-line live summary from /api/compute-balance; panels mount when opened; open state in localStorage `aiCompute.open.v1`. The FRED real-economy panels that used to sit there are RealEconomyPanels({ part }): power stays on Compute, money (investment, hyperscaler capex, AI debt, construction) is a collapsed block on Build-Out, labor (productivity, employment) a collapsed block on Dark Output
- [src/tabs/ai/GatewayShares.jsx](../../src/tabs/ai/GatewayShares.jsx): AI Economy → Tokens: managed inference through the Vercel AI Gateway: open-weights share of tokens and of spend by week, share by lab (tokens or spend), the lab table with price ratios and the same lab on OpenRouter, and the 15 most-used models
- [src/tabs/ai/ComputeBalance.jsx](../../src/tabs/ai/ComputeBalance.jsx): top of AI Economy → Compute: the shortage-or-glut board (each signal, its reading, grade, history and rule; click a row for what it means and where its chart is) and the growth race
- [src/tabs/RegimeMapTab.jsx](../../src/tabs/RegimeMapTab.jsx): Today → Regime Map (?view=regime&sub=markets|analogs): the page that holds Four markets and Analogs as two views; the view is owned by App.jsx so the Cockpit card can choose it
- [src/tabs/FourMarkets.jsx](../../src/tabs/FourMarkets.jsx): Regime Map → Four markets: regime, stance and clarity beside the ERP percentile, Fed-turn and 13-week curve KPIs, then links to ten sections that open and close (each with a one-line summary when closed; open state remembered per browser): one per market (chart read, breakout, signals with what each means for an equity book, and its instruments with breakout, relative strength and sparklines), the yield curve (regime, 2s10s chart with the regime band, every week since 1990, what followed each regime), the Fed turn (state, the three tells and the no-cuts-priced condition, every week since 1990, past blink and holding-firm episodes), the momentum leaderboard, the regime map, the playbook and the method
- [src/tabs/RegimeAnalogs.jsx](../../src/tabs/RegimeAnalogs.jsx): Regime Map → Analogs: the regime map (every 13-week window since 1990 as a node, coloured by regime, today and its ten analogs marked), the closest periods with what followed (click a row), why the selected analog matches reading by reading, the S&P fan chart after the analogs (median, middle half, 10th to 90th), analog outcomes against any week, similarity to past stress episodes, and the does-it-predict check
- [src/tabs/RegimeSummary.jsx](../../src/tabs/RegimeSummary.jsx): the Cockpit's regime card, a summary of the Regime Map page in six numbered blocks (the wind: the liquidity × growth plane; how sure: clarity on a stance bar; the Fed and the curve: the selloff trigger and three tells as a checklist; the four markets; the signal tape of all twelve signals; seen before: the analog regime, the three closest periods and the S&P fan). - [src/tabs/regime/shared.jsx](../../src/tabs/regime/shared.jsx): the colour maps for regimes, curve labels, Fed-turn states and trend states, plus Chip and ScoreBar, shared by the three files above
- [src/tabs/LiquidityTab.jsx](../../src/tabs/LiquidityTab.jsx): U.S. Economy → Rates & Fed → Liquidity (the default view there): verdict and key readings, a strip of the six layers linking to each, the composite and impulse with the lead test, net liquidity (moved here from FedSubTab.jsx, which keeps the accounts), and one collapsible section per layer listing every gauge with its grade, 26 weeks of grades, three years of history, rule and home
- [src/tabs/ai/RampAiIndex.jsx](../../src/tabs/ai/RampAiIndex.jsx): AI Economy → Tokens, under Census adoption: what businesses pay for AI (adoption and the lab mix, spend per employee on a log axis, AI's share of spend, token shares and blended price, adoption by industry and size)

## Skills
- `npm run dev`: Vite dev server on port 5180 (or `$PORT`), all interfaces
- `npm run build`: production bundle into `dist/`
- `npm run preview`: serve the built bundle
